Our Origins

The FreshMart Story: From Local Distributor to National FMCG Giant

Founded in 2012 in Gurugram, India, FreshMart FMCG began with a clear mission: to bridge the gap between world-class fast moving consumer goods manufacturers and the millions of neighborhood retail stores that form the backbone of the nation's commerce.

Over the past decade, we have expanded our portfolio to encompass 9 distinct categories including laundry care, shampoos, surface cleaners, pest repellents, luxury perfumes, and plush soft toys — operating state-of-the-art automated logistics centers across 50+ tier-1 and tier-2 cities.

Milestone History

Business Growth Timeline

A journey of continuous expansion, technology adoption, and customer satisfaction.

2012

Company Incorporation

FreshMart FMCG was founded in Gurugram with a single warehouse specializing in detergent powders and soaps.

Expansion into Personal Care & Cleaners

Partnered directly with major brands like Surf Excel, Harpic, Dove, and Sunsilk to expand product catalog to 150+ SKUs.
