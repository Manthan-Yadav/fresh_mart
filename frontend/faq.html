Frequently Asked Questions

Clear Answers

Common FMCG Queries

Click on any question below to expand the answer.

1. What is FreshMart FMCG and what products do you offer? ▼
FreshMart FMCG is India's leading fast moving consumer goods manufacturer and wholesale distributor. We supply 9 core essential categories: Detergent Powders, Shampoos, Adhesive Tapes, Toilet Cleaners, Dish Wash Bars, Mosquito Repellents, Perfumes, Rose Water, and Plush Soft Toys.
2. Do you supply products for both retail customers and bulk wholesale buyers? ▼
Yes, we cater to both ends of the supply chain! Individual retail buyers can browse our catalog, while store owners, Kirana merchants, supermarket chains, and corporate offices benefit from volume wholesale pricing tiers and distributor credit terms.
3. Are all your products 100% authentic and fresh from original manufacturers? ▼
Absolutely! Every brand in our inventory — including Surf Excel, Ariel, Tide, Dove, Harpic, Good Knight, Fogg, and Dabur — is sourced directly from certified original manufacturers. All products carry fresh manufacturing batch stamps with maximum shelf life.
4. How can I place a bulk order or request a wholesale distributor account? ▼
You can place a bulk inquiry directly through our website by adding items to the "Bulk Inquiry List" or by visiting our Contact page to fill out the distributor quotation form. Our corporate sales manager will respond with a formal invoice within 2 hours.
5. What are your standard delivery timelines and minimum order quantities (MOQ)? ▼
For metro cities, standard delivery takes 24 to 48 hours. For regional districts, dispatch takes 2 to 4 business days. The minimum order value for wholesale pricing is ₹5,000 across mixed category cartons.
6. What is your return or replacement policy for damaged shipments? ▼
We offer a hassle-free 7-day replacement guarantee. If any goods arrive with seal damage, transit leakages, or packaging defects, notify our support line with unboxing photos, and we will issue an immediate replacement or credit note.
7. How do you ensure hygiene and temperature control during warehousing and transit? ▼
Our distribution hubs adhere to ISO 9001:2015 and FSSAI hygiene standards. Chemical cleaners, perfumes, and personal care items are isolated in climate-controlled zones away from direct sunlight to preserve active chemical stability and scent fragrance.
8. Can I request product samples before placing a large commercial order? ▼
Yes! Verified business buyers, hotel chains, and retail store owners can request sample kits for detergent powders, perfumes, rose water, and surface cleaners through our customer desk.
9. How can I track my bulk shipment or logistics dispatch status? ▼
Once your order is dispatched from our central hub, you will receive an SMS and WhatsApp alert containing a real-time GPS tracking link for the logistics transport vehicle.
10. Do you offer private labeling or custom OEM packaging for FMCG items? ▼
Yes, FreshMart FMCG offers contract manufacturing and white-labeling services for corporate clients interested in launching their own brand of detergent powders, toilet cleaners, rose water, or adhesive tapes. Contact our OEM team for details.
